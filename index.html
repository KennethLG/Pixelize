Pixelize

Fake AI pixel art in, real pixel art out.

Choose an image

Start with an AI image that imitates pixel art: big blocky "pixels" of uneven size and slightly noisy colors. Pixelize finds the real grid and rebuilds the art at its true size.

  • Drop the file on the box, click it, or paste it with Ctrl+V.
  • PNG works best. A spritesheet with several frames is fine.
  • Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Find the pixel grid

The orange lines on the original mark each real pixel. If they line up with the blocks, go on. If not, adjust them here.
